### 🌟 KEY FEATURES 🌟

#### 🕶️ LIVE AR PRICE SCANNER & FLOATING OVERLAYS
Point your iPhone or iPad camera at any LEGO® box, loose set, rare minifigure, or collectible card. HelloBrick's computer vision engine instantly recognizes the item and projects live floating market values, sealed vs. used price pills, and condition grades directly over your items in augmented reality.

Scanning an entire shelf or collection? Point your camera to detect multiple sets at once. View a live combined batch total and add all detected items to your digital vault with a single tap.
