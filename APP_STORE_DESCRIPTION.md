Are you a LEGO® collector, investor, or enthusiast? HelloBrick is the ultimate LEGO® scanner, value tracker, and portfolio manager designed to help you organize and value your LEGO® bricks, LEGO® sets, and LEGO® minifigures instantly!

Stop guessing what your mixed LEGO® collection is worth. With HelloBrick's powerful AI LEGO® scanner, you can scan any LEGO® set box or LEGO® minifig in seconds to instantly identify it, reveal its current market value, and add it to your digital LEGO® vault!

Whether you collect rare Star Wars LEGO® minifigures, classic LEGO® Castle sets, or massive LEGO® Creator sets, HelloBrick helps you track real-time value and grow your custom LEGO® portfolio.

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES 🌟

#### 📷 INSTANT LEGO® SCANNER
Point your camera at any LEGO® box or LEGO® set instructions. Our high-precision AI instantly recognizes the set and matches it against our extensive database of tens of thousands of LEGO® sets. No manual typing, no barcode searching required.

#### 🧑 RARE LEGO® MINIFIGURE IDENTIFIER
Scan and identify over 15,000+ unique LEGO® minifigures! Our advanced computer vision scanner detects your custom minifigures, details their exact set history, and shows current Used vs. New prices. Now you can easily catalog your Cloud City Boba Fett, rare knights, or super heroes!

#### 📈 REAL-TIME LEGO® COLLECTION VALUE TRACKER
Know exactly what your LEGO® collection is worth right now. We pull live market pricing from across the secondary LEGO® market to provide accurate valuations for both Sealed (New) and Used LEGO® sets. Toggle currencies and track daily dynamic shifts.

#### 📊 VISUALIZE PORTFOLIO GROWTH
Watch your LEGO® collection grow! View dynamic SVG charts of your asset valuation over time, calculate your Return on Investment (ROI) percentages, and analyze your portfolio distribution by theme (Lego City, Star Wars, Technic, Marvel, MOCs, and more).

#### 🏆 GLOBAL COLLECTORS LEADERBOARD
See how you rank among top LEGO® collectors worldwide! Climb the ranks as you scan more sets and add rare minifigures to your portfolio. It’s the ultimate gamification for serious LEGO® investors.
