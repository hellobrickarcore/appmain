#### 📷 HIGH-PRECISION AI SET SCANNER
Stop manual typing. Just point your camera at any set box, instruction manual, or built model. Our advanced computer vision scanner instantly identifies the exact set and matches it against our global database of tens of thousands of items in seconds.

#### 🕶️ REVOLUTIONARY AR VALUE LENS
Experience the future. Activate our custom Augmented Reality mode and point your lens at your shelf. HelloBrick projects beautiful 3D glassmorphic cards in real-time, displaying live resale market valuations, rarity indexes, and price changes floating right over your sets.

#### 📈 LIVE PORTFOLIO DYNAMICS (SEALED vs. USED)
Watch your net worth grow. HelloBrick tracks secondary collector markets daily to give you highly accurate, real-time valuations. Toggle between Sealed (New) and Used prices to evaluate your collection based on its true physical condition.

Analyze your collection like a professional index fund. View gorgeous interactive price charts, calculate your precise Return on Investment (ROI) percentages, and inspect your portfolio asset allocation broken down by themes (Star Wars, Castle, Space, Technic, and more).
